# Community Code of Conduct

We want participation in UAAOS – MARGDARSHAN to be respectful, constructive and safe.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and patient with people of different experience levels.
- Critique ideas and technical decisions, not people.
- Provide clear, reproducible and good-faith feedback.
- Respect privacy; do not publish another person's private information.
- Credit original authors and respect licenses and attribution requirements.
- Disclose uncertainty and distinguish evidence from opinion.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, threats, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Publishing secrets, private data or identifying information without permission.
- Deliberately misleading contributors or fabricating test results.
- Spam, impersonation or malicious links and files.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, close or remove content and restrict participation when necessary to protect the community. To report misconduct, contact the repository maintainer through a private channel available on the hosting platform. Do not include sensitive details in a public issue.
